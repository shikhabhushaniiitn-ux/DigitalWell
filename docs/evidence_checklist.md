# Final project evidence checklist

Capture these images yourself from the running app. They are demonstration evidence, not usability results. Remove names, personal notifications, account identifiers and private browsing information. Device screenshots must be genuine; SVG illustrations do not depict device interfaces.

The app also contains labeled official Google product-reference screenshots. They illustrate product interfaces only; do not count them as your device setup, product testing, usability evidence, or accessibility review.

A reachable URL confirms an HTTP response at the check time, not the accuracy or safety of its content.
