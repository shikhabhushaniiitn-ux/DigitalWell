# DigitalWell image credits and asset register

Photographs are stored locally under `assets/images/`; the site does not depend on remote image hosts when it runs. Nine photos were added for Sprint 5. The existing Julio Lopez hero photo remains in the root of `assets/images/`. The new JPEGs were downloaded from Unsplash image assets, cropped to 1200 × 900 pixels, and optimized as progressive JPEGs (quality 82). Accessed 11 November 2026.

Each photo page below marked the image “Free to use under the Unsplash License” when reviewed. Unsplash states its license permits free commercial and non-commercial use and does not require attribution, though attribution is appreciated: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The license concerns copyright in the photo; separate rights may apply to identifiable people, brands, private property, or other depicted works. DigitalWell uses the photographs in a positive educational context and does not imply endorsement. No separate model or property releases were independently reviewed.
