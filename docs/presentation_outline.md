## Slide 2 — The student problem
- Connected devices support study and social life, but can compete for attention.
- Students need approachable ways to explore options and compare trade-offs.

## Slide 3 — Objectives and audience
- Help students find, compare, and understand source-linked resources.
- Audience: students exploring focus, screen habits, sleep, privacy, or safety.

- Compare two to four choices across purpose, platforms, cost, privacy, accessibility, advantages, and limitations.

## Slide 7 — Topics, Resource Finder, and Planner
- Four pathways: focus, screen-time balance, sleep, and privacy.
- Transparent fixed-rule suggestions connect goals to resources and guides, then offer a session-only action checklist and reflection.
- Daily Routine Planner drafts a non-overlapping study day from the student's preferred times; it does not save a profile.
- Screenshots: `07_resource_finder.png`, `14_daily_routine_planner.png`, `15_guided_action_plan.png`.
