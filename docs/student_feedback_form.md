# DigitalWell student usability feedback

Use with 3–5 student volunteers when available. Participation should be voluntary. Explain the purpose before starting, ask permission to take anonymized notes, and let the student stop at any time. Do not collect names, account details, browsing history, diagnoses or health information.

## Tasks

1. Find a resource that could help you reduce study distractions. Describe one detail you would check before installing it.
2. Compare two resources. Identify one difference and one detail that is unverified or not stated.
3. Follow one setup guide. Find the first action you would take and where you would go if the menu labels differ on your device.

## Five feedback questions

1. How easy was it to find the section you needed? What helped or slowed you down?
2. Were the resource purpose, platform and cost details clear? What was confusing?
3. Did the comparison make differences and unknown information easy to understand?
4. Were the setup guide steps easy to follow for your device or browser? What would you change?
5. What is the one improvement that would make DigitalWell more useful or comfortable to use?
