# Student user-testing plan

## Purpose

Check whether students can find an appropriate resource, understand comparison caveats, and follow a setup guide. This is a usability exercise, not research about health outcomes.

## Participants

Invite 3–5 adult student volunteers who use different devices where possible. Explain the purpose, expected time (about 15 minutes), what feedback will be recorded, and that participation is voluntary. Do not collect names, account details, browsing history, health information or screenshots containing personal data. Ask permission before taking notes; let a participant stop at any time. Follow applicable school/college requirements.

## Tasks

1. On Home, find the section that helps reduce study distractions.
2. Search for an Android screen-time option and filter to Android resources.
3. Compare two tools and explain one difference and one unknown/limitation.
4. Open the LeechBlock guide and identify what information to enter and where to save it.
5. Find the online safety and privacy learning resources.

Observe completion, points of confusion, time only if useful, and participant comments. Do not coach during a task unless they ask or become uncomfortable. Ask: “What was clear?”, “What was confusing?”, and “What would you change first?”
