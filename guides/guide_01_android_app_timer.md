## Introduction
An app timer can make your daily use of a selected app more visible and set a boundary. The steps below follow Google's Android Help instructions; menu names and availability can differ by device, Android version, manufacturer and managed account.

## Requirements
- An Android phone with Digital Wellbeing available.
- Access to Settings and the app you want to set a limit for.
- Some work or school accounts may not support app timers.

## Before you start
Choose one app whose use you want to make more intentional. Pick an initial limit that is realistic for your classes, work and social needs; you can review and change it later.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** on your phone.
2. Tap **Digital Wellbeing**. If this is your first time, follow the on-screen setup.
3. Find the app you want to limit in the usage chart. Tap **Show all apps** if needed.
4. Tap the timer control next to the app (often **Set timer** or **App timer**).
5. Choose a daily time allowance and confirm with **OK** or the displayed save control.
6. Revisit Digital Wellbeing later to see whether the limit fits your needs.

## Practical tip
Start with one app and check the usage chart after a few days. A timer is a reminder and boundary you control; it is not a measure of personal success or failure.

## Expected result
When the selected limit is reached, Android may close or pause the app and dim its icon. Timers generally reset at midnight. You can remove or adjust a timer in the same settings area.

## Troubleshooting
- **Digital Wellbeing is missing:** search Settings for “Digital Wellbeing” or check whether your device supports it.
- **No timer control appears:** update the device where possible, check the app list, and note that managed work/school accounts can restrict timers.
- **The menu labels differ:** use Settings search or consult your device maker's support instructions.
- **The limit feels too strict:** adjust it or remove it; keep access to essential communication, health, study and emergency apps.
