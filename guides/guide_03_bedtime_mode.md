## Introduction
Android Bedtime mode can apply a scheduled routine and optional screen or notification changes. Set it to a time that supports your routine, and keep essential contacts and alarms available. Device labels and options vary.

## Requirements
- An Android phone with Digital Wellbeing and Bedtime mode available.
- A bedtime and wake-up schedule, or the charging window you want to use.
- Optional: review notification filtering before using Do Not Disturb.

## Before you start
Choose days and times that fit your actual routine. Consider which people or apps should still be able to reach you, and check how your alarm behaves before relying on the schedule.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings** and tap **Digital Wellbeing**.
2. Tap **Bedtime mode**, then **Bedtime routine** (labels may vary).
3. Choose when Bedtime mode should start.
4. Select **Use a schedule** and choose start/end times and days, or choose the charging-based routine if offered.
5. Review optional settings such as Do Not Disturb, grayscale, dim wallpaper or dark theme. Keep any needed calls and alarms allowed.
6. Save or confirm the routine, then check the next scheduled start in Settings.
7. Use Quick Settings to turn Bedtime mode on or off temporarily if your device provides that shortcut.

## Practical tip
Try the routine for a few nights and adjust its start time or optional screen settings if they do not suit you. Bedtime mode changes device behavior; it does not guarantee better sleep.

## Expected result
At the scheduled time, Bedtime mode applies the selected settings. Depending on the configuration, it may turn off at the scheduled end, at the next alarm, or when charging stops.

## Troubleshooting
- **Bedtime mode not found:** search Settings or look under Modes on newer Android versions.
- **Schedule does not activate:** check selected weekdays, start/end times and any charging condition.
- **Important alerts are silenced:** inspect Do Not Disturb filters and allow the contacts/apps you need.
- **Options differ:** follow your device maker's documentation; menu names and features vary across versions.
